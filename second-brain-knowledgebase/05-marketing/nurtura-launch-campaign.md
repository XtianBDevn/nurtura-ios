# Nurtura Launch Campaign

## Campaign Name
Care, Naturally

## Objective
Generate the first 1,000 qualified waitlist signups and 150 TestFlight beta
participants within 8 weeks, with at least 30 testers using the Apple Watch app.

## Audience
Family caregivers and adult children coordinating care for an aging parent or
loved one, plus a secondary segment of professional caregivers who need simple
daily documentation and coordination.

## Core Offer
Join the Nurtura beta and help shape a calmer care coordination app for iPhone
and Apple Watch.

## Funnel

1. Awareness: caregiver pain-point posts, short videos, partner mentions.
2. Consideration: landing page, screenshots, privacy FAQ, Apple Watch demo.
3. Conversion: beta signup form.
4. Activation: TestFlight invite, onboarding checklist.
5. Advocacy: tester feedback, testimonials, referral invites.

## Email Sequence

### Email 1: Welcome
Subject: Welcome to calmer care coordination

Body:
Thanks for joining the Nurtura beta list. We’re building Nurtura for the quiet
daily work of care: medications, reminders, notes, schedules, updates, and the
small details that often get scattered across texts.

CTA: Tell us what you coordinate today

### Email 2: Problem
Subject: The group chat was not built for care

Body:
Care needs context. Who gave the medication? What changed today? When is the
next appointment? Nurtura brings those moments into one shared place.

CTA: See the care flow

### Email 3: Watch App
Subject: Care reminders, right on your wrist

Body:
The Nurtura Watch app is designed for quick checks: what is next, what needs
attention, and what was already handled.

CTA: Join the Watch beta group

### Email 4: Privacy
Subject: Care information should stay careful

Body:
Nurtura is designed around sensitive care information. We use fictional demo
data in marketing, avoid ad targeting based on health details, and keep privacy
plain.

CTA: Read the privacy FAQ

### Email 5: Beta Invite
Subject: Your Nurtura beta invite

Body:
You’re invited to test Nurtura on iPhone and Apple Watch. We’re looking for
feedback on onboarding, medication routines, schedules, care recipient setup,
and Watch sync.

CTA: Open TestFlight
