# Nurtura 1-90 Day Marketing Guide

## Positioning

Nurtura helps caregivers turn scattered care tasks into a calm daily system.

Primary audience:

- Family caregivers caring for a parent, spouse, or child.
- Professional caregivers managing shifts, logs, medications, and schedules.

Do not position Nurtura as medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, HIPAA compliance, or emergency monitoring.

## Core Message

```text
Nurtura helps families and caregivers organize daily care, medications, schedules, and quality-of-life notes in one simple app.
```

## Days 1-7: Foundation

Goals:

- Make the product credible.
- Recruit first testers.
- Collect language from real users.

Actions:

- Create a simple landing page with email capture.
- Add screenshots from the simulator and later from TestFlight.
- Write a plain privacy policy and support page.
- Recruit 10 caregivers for TestFlight.
- Post founder story on LinkedIn and local community groups.
- Ask each tester for one quote about the hardest part of caregiving.

Content:

- "Why caregiver coordination breaks down"
- "How to track care without a giant spreadsheet"
- "What we learned from our first caregiver testers"

## Days 8-30: Beta Learning

Goals:

- Get 25 to 50 testers.
- Improve onboarding.
- Find strongest use case.

Actions:

- Run weekly TestFlight builds.
- Track which screens testers understand without help.
- Create short demo videos:
  - Add a care recipient.
  - Create a care plan.
  - Log a care activity.
  - Track a shift.
- Reach out to:
  - caregiver Facebook groups
  - senior-care newsletters
  - local home-care agencies
  - elder-law attorneys
  - discharge planners

Metrics:

- testers invited
- testers installed
- onboarding completion rate
- first care recipient created
- first care log created
- tester feedback count

## Days 31-60: Launch Preparation

Goals:

- Prepare App Store launch.
- Build repeatable acquisition.
- Create trust assets.

Actions:

- Finalize App Store screenshots and description.
- Publish help articles:
  - Getting started
  - Adding a loved one
  - Understanding the care plan
  - Sharing care with a team
- Create a 60-second product walkthrough.
- Build email sequence:
  - Welcome
  - How to set up a recipient
  - How to use daily logs
  - How to keep family informed
- Build a list of 100 referral partners:
  - geriatric care managers
  - home-care agencies
  - senior centers
  - caregiver coaches
  - elder-law firms

## Days 61-90: Public Launch

Goals:

- Launch publicly.
- Convert early users into advocates.
- Learn what users will pay for.

Actions:

- Announce on email list.
- Post launch video.
- Ask testers for App Store reviews after they have a successful care log.
- Run small paid tests only after organic messaging is clear.
- Partner with 3 to 5 caregiver organizations for demos.
- Offer concierge onboarding calls for first 25 serious users.

Paid Experiment Ideas:

- Google Search: "caregiver app", "medication tracker for parents", "elder care organizer".
- Meta: adult children caring for parents.
- Local sponsorship: caregiver support newsletters.

Do not spend heavily until activation is proven.

## Weekly Operating Cadence

Every Monday:

- Review installs, onboarding completion, and first-log completion.
- Pick one funnel problem.

Every Wednesday:

- Ship one app improvement or one piece of content.

Every Friday:

- Talk to two caregivers.
- Save exact phrases they use.

## Success Metrics By Day 90

Strong result:

- 500+ installs.
- 150+ onboarded users.
- 75+ users with at least one care recipient.
- 50+ users with at least one care log.
- 20+ users using the app weekly.
- 10+ meaningful testimonials or case studies.

If results are weaker, do not panic. Find where the funnel breaks:

- People do not install: message/channel problem.
- People install but do not onboard: onboarding problem.
- People onboard but do not log: value/timing problem.
- People log once but do not return: habit/notification/team problem.

## Launch Copy Bank

Short description:

```text
Nurtura is a caregiving app for organizing loved ones, care logs, medications, schedules, and quality-of-life notes in one place.
```

Social post:

```text
Caregiving gets messy fast: medications, appointments, notes, family updates, and daily tasks. Nurtura brings it into one calmer system. We are opening TestFlight spots for caregivers who want to help shape the app.
```

Tester invite:

```text
We are testing Nurtura, a new iPhone app for caregivers. If you help care for a parent, spouse, child, or client, we would love your feedback. It takes about 10 minutes to try the setup flow.
```
